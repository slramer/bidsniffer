La Alma Lincoln Improvements

La Alma-Lincoln Park is a historic 15.4-acre park located within Denver's La Alma-Lincoln Park neighborhood and designated Historic Cultural District. As one of Denver's most culturally significant public spaces, the park serves as a center for recreation, community gathering, cultural expression, and neighborhood identity. The City and County of Denver is undertaking a comprehensive renovation of the park to modernize aging infrastructure, improve accessibility and safety, preserve historic assets, and create a more vibrant and inclusive destination for residents and visitors of all ages. The project includes improvements throughout the park, including rehabilitation and enhancement of the historic amphitheater, construction of a Cultural Story Loop highlighting the neighborhood's rich history and cultural heritage, a new cultural plaza featuring a custom shade structure designed to support community events and gatherings, expanded skate amenities, new playground facilities, upgraded sports courts, a roller loop, enhanced picnic and gathering spaces, landscape and irrigation improvements, site furnishings, lighting, accessibility upgrades, stormwater and drainage improvements, and other related site infrastructure. Collectively, these improvements will transform La Alma-Lincoln Park into a more connected, welcoming, and resilient public space while honoring the historic character and cultural legacy that make the park a treasured community asset.
