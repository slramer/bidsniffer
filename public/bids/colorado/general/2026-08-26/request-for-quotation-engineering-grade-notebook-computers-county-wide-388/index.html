REQUEST FOR QUOTATION Engineering-Grade Notebook Computers -- County-Wide Clear Creek County, Georgetown, Colorado Issuing Department: Clear Creek County IT Department RFQ Number: RFQ #IT-2026-006 Issue Date: August 26, 2026 Submission Deadline: September 4, 2026 at 5:00 PM MDT Submission Method: Email only -- see Section 5 for instructions Contact / Submit To: For security reasons, you must enable JavaScript to view this E-mail address. Award Basis: Best overall value -- price is one factor among several 1. Purpose and Overview Clear Creek County IT Department is soliciting competitive quotations from qualified vendors for the procurement of engineering-grade notebook computers for county-wide use. This is an open public request; any qualified vendor may respond. The County requires high-performance mobile workstations capable of supporting demanding technical and professional workloads including GIS, design, data analysis, and field computing. Units must feature dedicated professional-grade graphics, vPro manageability, Copilot+ AI capability, and enterprise-grade wireless connectivity. This RFQ is not a contract or a commitment to purchase. The County reserves the right to accept or reject any or all quotations, to waive any informalities, and to award to the vendor deemed most advantageous to the County. 2. Deployment Context Vendors should understand the intended use when preparing proposals: - County-wide deployment across multiple departments requiring engineering-grade mobile computing - Intended for technical, analytical, and professional workloads -- GIS, design, data processing, field computing - vPro technology required for remote management and out-of-band IT administration - Copilot+ AI platform required for Microsoft AI feature compatibility - Dedicated professional GPU required -- integrated graphics not acceptable for this application - Wi-Fi 7 required for next-generation wireless infrastructure compatibility - All units must be new; no refurbished or gray-market units 3. Technical Specifications Vendors must quote the exact model specified below, or may propose an approved equivalent with full technical documentation attached. Substitutions must be clearly identified with a side-by-side specification comparison demonstrating equivalent or superior performance, manageability, and GPU capability. Item 1 -- Dell Pro Max MC16250 16" Copilot+ PC Notebook (Qty: 3) Specification Requirement Make / Model Dell Pro Max MC16250 16" Copilot+ PC Notebook (or approved equivalent) Processor Intel Core Ultra 9 285H, vPro Technology Platform Copilot+ PC (Microsoft AI-capable platform) Memory 32 GB Storage 1 TB SSD Display 16" Full HD Plus, 1920 x 1200, 60 Hz Operating System Windows 11 Pro Graphics NVIDIA RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell, 8 GB dedicated Camera Front Camera / Webcam Wireless IEEE 802.11be Wireless LAN Standard (Wi-Fi 7) Keyboard English (US) Warranty / Support Dell ProSupport + Next Business Day Onsite, 5 Years (10x5) Quantity 3 units Condition New only -- no refurbished or gray-market units Pricing Response Format Vendors must provide itemized pricing in the format below.
