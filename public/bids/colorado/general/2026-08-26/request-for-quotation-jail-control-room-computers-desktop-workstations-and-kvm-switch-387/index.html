REQUEST FOR QUOTATION Jail Control Room Computers -- Desktop Workstations & KVM Switch Clear Creek County Detention Facility, Georgetown, Colorado Issuing Department: Clear Creek County Detention Facility / IT Department RFQ Number: RFQ #IT-2026-005 Issue Date: August 26, 2026 Submission Deadline: Sept 4, 2026 at 5:00 PM MDT Submission Method: Email only -- see Section 5 for instructions Contact / Submit To: For security reasons, you must enable JavaScript to view this E-mail address. Award Basis: Best overall value -- price is one factor among several 1. Purpose and Overview The Clear Creek County Detention Facility, in coordination with the Clear Creek County IT Department, is soliciting competitive quotations from qualified vendors for the procurement of desktop computer workstations and a KVM switch for use in the jail control room. This is an open public request; any qualified vendor may respond. The control room environment requires high-performance, enterprise-grade desktop computers capable of supporting multiple simultaneous display outputs, sustained workloads, and dedicated graphics for jail management, surveillance, and monitoring systems. A 4-port KVM switch is required to share peripherals across workstations. This RFQ is not a contract or a commitment to purchase. The County reserves the right to accept or reject any or all quotations, to waive any informalities, and to award to the vendor deemed most advantageous to the County. 2. Deployment Context Vendors should understand the intended environment when preparing proposals: - Location: Clear Creek County Detention Facility, Georgetown, CO 80444 - Use case: Jail control room -- primary operator workstations and supporting secondary desktops - All units will operate in a secure, controlled facility environment with 24/7 uptime expectations - Primary workstations (FCT2250) require dedicated GPU for multi-monitor and surveillance system support - Secondary workstations (QBS1250) are slim-form-factor general-use units for supporting staff - KVM switch required to share keyboard, mouse, and DisplayPort displays between workstations - All equipment must be new; no refurbished or gray-market units 3. Technical Specifications Vendors must quote the exact models specified below, or may propose approved equivalents with full technical documentation attached. Substitutions must be clearly identified with a side-by-side specification comparison.
