Town of Frederick Arts Committee Frederick Engineering Building Mural Project Call for Artists

The Town of Frederick Arts Committee is pleased to announce the Frederick Engineering Building Mural Project and invites artists to submit designs for consideration for a new public mural at the Town's Engineering Building. The mural will celebrate Frederick's history, present-day community, and future through a bright, vibrant, and inclusive work of public art. The selected design should reflect the character and identity of Frederick and create a welcoming and visually engaging addition to the community. Please see attached and related documents for more details. Deadline for Submission All submissions must be received by the Town Clerk's Office no later than: October 20, 2026 at 5:00 P.M. Late submissions may not be considered.
