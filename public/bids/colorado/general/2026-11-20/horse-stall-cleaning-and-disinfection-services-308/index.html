Horse Stall Cleaning and Disinfection Services

The Montrose County Fair Grounds and Event Center is soliciting proposals from qualified businesses or individuals to provide horse stall cleaning and disinfection services on an asneeded basis at the Montrose County Fairgrounds & Event Center, located at 1001 North 2nd Street, Montrose, Colorado. The Fairgrounds & Event Center strives to maintain a clean, safe, and well-maintained facility for equine exhibitors, participants, and guests. Horse stalls are utilized throughout the year for scheduled equine events and require thorough cleaning and disinfection between uses to support animal health, facility cleanliness, and event readiness. The selected contractor will work in coordination with Fairgrounds staff to complete cleaning assignments within established event schedules and turnaround windows.
