RFP: Aztlan Outdoor Pool Replacement CM/GC

The City and County of Denver is seeking a Construction Manager/General Contractor to provide services for the Aztlan Outdoor Pool Replacement project located at 4435 Navajo St, Denver, CO 80211. The purpose of this project is to replace the existing lap and kiddie pools, related infrastructure, pool mechanical and plumbing systems, and the stand-alone pool equipment building, all of which have reached the end of their useful life. The project will include a 25-meter lap pool with an integrated zero-entry kiddie pool and splash pad. A community-informed water feature will also be incorporated into the project. Improvements to the recreation facility locker rooms will be included to bring these spaces into compliance with current building codes and accessibility standards. The extent of these improvements will be determined during design by the Owner and Design Team.
