RFP: 2027 City Lots and Garages Professional Parking Management Svcs

The Department of Transportation and Infrastructure, and Denver Arts and Venues, controls and operates certain public parking facilities for the City and County of Denver (CCD). The purpose of this request is to secure a qualified and responsive facility contractor to provide professional management services for the operation and maintenance of the three (3) City parking garages and seven (7) City surface parking lots. A Pre-Proposal Meeting will be held on September 15, 2026, at 1:00 p.m., local time. To access the meeting, please call 720-388-6219 and enter the conference ID: 743-227-714# or join via Microsoft Teams . Contractors are responsible for any information conveyed at this meeting.
