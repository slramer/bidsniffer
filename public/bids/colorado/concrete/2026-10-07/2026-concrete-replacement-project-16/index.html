2026 Concrete Replacement Project

This project consists of the removal and replacement of existing concrete flatwork, curb, gutter, sidewalk, trough drains, driveway pans, curb ramps, fillets and asphalt in various locations in Fruita. In addition, removal and replacement of existing unsuitable subgrade material may be required at the discretion of the project manager. Additional items, including but not limited to traffic control, stormwater management materials testing, shall be required of the contractor. A non-mandatory, pre-bid meeting with prospective Offerors will be held on October 19, 2026, at 1:30 PM at the Fruita Civic Center located at 325 E. Aspen Ave. in Fruita, Colorado.
