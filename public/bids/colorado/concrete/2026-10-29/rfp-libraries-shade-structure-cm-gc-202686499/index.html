RFP: Libraries + Shade Structure CM/GC

The project has three distinct scopes of work. - The Blair-Caldwell African American Research Library and Museum Renovation will be a full renovation of the 2 nd and 3 rd floors including but not limited to reconfiguration of the 2 nd floor research library, the 3 rd floor museum, staff workplace renovation, renovation of Special Collections, restrooms, and structural modifications to support the current use of these spaces. - The Montbello Branch Library Renovation will be a comprehensive renovation of the space, end-of-life building system replacements, and the design of a new outdoor space adjacent to the library but independent of the Montbello Shade Structure project. - The Montbello Civic Center Park scope includes a shaded cultural plaza for events, picnicking, and gatherings. Vibrant Denver Bond Program : Approved by voters in 2025, the $950 million Vibrant Denver Bond Program is delivering 58 major projects across Denver, including libraries, parks and recreation centers, cultural facilities, affordable housing, improved streets and bridges, a family health clinic, a children&rsquo;s advocacy center, and much more &ndash; without adding any new taxes. This bond program was informed by direct community input from thousands of Denverites and community leaders. The City will build the projects in this bond over six years from 2026 &ndash; 2031.
