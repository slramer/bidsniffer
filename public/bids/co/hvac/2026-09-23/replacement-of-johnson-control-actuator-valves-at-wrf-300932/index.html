Replacement of Johnson Control Actuator Valves at WRF

The City of Pueblo is seeking a qualified commercial plumbing or mechanical contractor to replace eight obsolete Johnson Controls actuator valves serving the hot-water circulation loop in the Administration Building at the Wastewater Reclamation Facility (WRF). The project requires compatible replacement valves&mdash;Johnson Controls Series M100 or approved equal&mdash;with 120V power and 24V transformer/thermostat control.
