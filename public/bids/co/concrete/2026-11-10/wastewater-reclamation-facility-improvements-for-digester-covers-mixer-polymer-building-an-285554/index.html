Wastewater Reclamation Facility Improvements for Digester Covers, Mixer, Polymer Building, and Electrical

- In general, this project will consist of improvements to the Wastewater Reclamation Facility (WRF) including: - New electrical and instrumentation improvements - HVAC replacements and modifications - Upgrades to the digester mixing systems - Placement of two digester covers - Improvements to the solids area polymer system - Sitework also includes buried piping demolition, pavement demolition and replacement, and construction of a new cast-in-place foundation - The digester covers and limited special services will be purchased by the City under separate contract, with Contractor providing the balance of work beyond the scope of the cover manufacturer. - Contractor shall perform all work as detailed in the Invitation for Bid, plans, and specifications included in the project documents.
