Roadway and Signalization Improvements - State Highway 45A (Pueblo Boulevard)

In general, this project will consist of Brief Description , as detailed in the plans and specifications. This project also includes the installation of raised medians on Pueblo Boulevard approximately 1200 feet to the east and 2000 feet to the west of Mirror Avenue. The raised medians will include a 6-inch over-excavation of native fill below existing asphalt and replacement with Class 6 ABC. The east bound to north bound left turn lane will receive a 2-inch mill and overlay with new striping and signage and other incidental items.
