West Side Improvements - Bus Stops Package 1

In general, this project will consist of the rehabilitation of 23 existing bus stops on the west side of Pueblo, including new concrete pads, curb and gutter, sidewalks, upgraded curb ramps, replacement of existing benches at select locations, installation of new benches where none currently exist, designated alighting areas, and illuminated bus stop signs as detailed in the plans and specifications for this project. Bus Stop Package 1 is a distinct component of the larger West Side Improvements Project and includes only the work associated with the locations identified within this package. These enhancements are intended to improve safety, accessibility, and ADA compliance, providing safer and more functional transit facilities for riders.
