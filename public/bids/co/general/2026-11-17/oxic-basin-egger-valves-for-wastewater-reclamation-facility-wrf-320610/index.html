Oxic Basin Egger Valves for Wastewater Reclamation Facility (WRF)

The City of Pueblo is seeking a qualified Contractor to provide materials and labor for the Oxic Reactor Basin, we are seeking better air flow control then the current Butterfly valve can attain. We want to use Iris valves for better combination pressure reducing and pressure sustaining control for the Water Treatment Plant. The work includes, but is not limited to, the removal of an existing 8 inch ABZ butterfly combination pressure reducing and pressure sustaining valve for a 8 inch pipe within the existing City of Pueblo, Water Treatment Plant; providing and installing a new 6 inch Egger Brand iris combination pressure reducing and pressure sustaining valve; removal of existing adjoining piping and fittings; providing and installing new adjoining piping and fittings; removal of existing appurtenances; providing and installing new appurtenances; and supports. The quoted prices must include Contractor providing all supervision, insurance, bonds, equipment, labor, materials and appurtenances, as needed, shown, or specified in this Invitation for Bid (IFB).
