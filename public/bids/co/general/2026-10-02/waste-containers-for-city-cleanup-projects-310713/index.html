Waste Containers for City Cleanup Projects

In general, the successful bidder will furnish, deliver, place, remove, transport, and properly dispose of collected material from 30-yard and 40-yard roll-off waste containers for City special cleanup projects conducted within the City of Pueblo city limits. Submitted prices are to include all charges for labor, administration, materials, supplies, shipping/handling, unloading, loading, delivery, fuel, insurance, and equipment necessary to supply the commodity as listed within this IFB.
