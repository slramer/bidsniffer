2027-2029 Emergency Sanitary Sewer Point Repair

The purpose of this project is to create an approved contractor list for emergency sanitary sewer point repairs, on an as needed basis. Emergency sanitary sewer point repairs include repairing sanitary sewer mains, supplying all materials, and providing traffic control for each project as contracted. Each contract (site) will be awarded as the need arises, based upon availability and project calculations from submitted unit prices. The term of the approved contractor list is three years, consisting of 2027, 2028, and 2029 with the option to renew for two additional one-year terms.
