FY2026 North Manhole Rehabilitation Project

In general, this project will consist of rehabilitating existing manholes throughout the City of Pueblo by placing calcium aluminate and epoxy coating. Specifically, the rehabilitation will include placing 159 vertical feet of epoxy coating and 159 vertical feet of calcium aluminate patching of existing manholes, rebuilding 11 manhole benches and troughs with fast-setting cementitious material, and removing and replacing 1 manhole ring and lid .
